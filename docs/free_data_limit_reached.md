# Free Data Limit Reached

The final zero-cost remediation preserves the strict point-in-time S&P 500 universe contract.

## Resolved

- SEC acquisition now requires an operator-supplied descriptive user agent, uses bounded retries,
  caching, and official bulk-first identity resources.
- Post-seed membership changes are reconstructed from explicit effective-dated events.
- Membership conflicts receive explicit evidence statuses.
- Price audits are resumable and cover effective-dated ticker intervals without a sample cap.
- Coverage-derived evaluation intervals require at least 156 contiguous monthly decisions.

## Remaining Limit

The current execution environment has no compliant `SEC_USER_AGENT` and no optional free Alpha
Vantage key. More importantly, zero-cost providers do not guarantee historical prices and economic
terminal values for every old delisted constituent. A final available close is not treated as a
delisting return, and missing terminal values are never replaced with zero.

No fully covered canonical interval has yet been established. A separately preregistered,
noncanonical research universe may be possible if its exclusions and coverage dates are frozen
before model work, but it must not be described as canonical S&P 500 point-in-time evidence.

Institutional or appropriately licensed historical constituent, delisting, and terminal-value data
would be required for a canonical claim if the remaining evidence cannot be recovered from free
official sources. Model training remains prohibited.
