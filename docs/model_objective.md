# Model Objective

The consolidated project targets:

> A long-only, point-in-time individual-stock ranking and portfolio system that rebalances
> periodically and attempts to outperform SPY Buy & Hold after costs on unseen and prospective
> data.

SPY Buy & Hold is the raw-return benchmark. Secondary metrics may evaluate drawdown, volatility,
turnover, concentration, and behavioural difficulty, but they do not redefine the benchmark.

A model is useful only if its inputs were knowable at each decision time, its comparison was
registered and reproducible, costs were included, and performance survives unseen and then
prospective observation. Historical fit, a higher in-sample Sharpe ratio, or a shadow prediction
record is not evidence of reliable prospective outperformance.

The current repository provides foundations and comparators. It does not claim that any model has
met the final objective.
